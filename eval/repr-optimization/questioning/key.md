## Nuance markers (which cases separate letter-following from real understanding)

- C1 vs C2: both are "should I ask?" — the discriminator is derivable-with-evidence (don't ask)
  vs genuinely-human-owned (ask). A reader who asks in both, or neither, missed the doctrine.
- C3 distractors (a) silent-switch and (d) why-less-ask are both plausible-sounding but wrong;
  the doctrine's "surface a better option WITH the reason, human chooses" is the narrow correct path.
- C5 vs C6: same "how to ask" axis, opposite ends of the prior-knowledge calibration. Swapping
  them (Socratic for the novice, worked-example for the expert) is the classic failure.
- C9 (a) and (b) are the "be agreeable" traps the doctrine explicitly forbids.
